Holding a unit costs 1 a week. A lost sale costs 3. Orders take two weeks to land. Lowest total cost wins.

Both agents are a few lines of JavaScript, not the real ones. Demand is random every game. The warehouse game shows the bullwhip effect: how much harder your orders swing than demand does.
